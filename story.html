The Beginning

Born from
the land

UNCMN began as an honest question: why is something this extraordinary — cacay oil, grown in Colombia, used by communities for generations — virtually unknown to the world?

The answer was supply chains too long, intermediaries too many, and stories untold. We decided to change that.

Traceability

Nothing
hidden

Full traceability is not a trend for us — it is a baseline. We know the region, the harvest, the press date, and the hands that touched your product at every step.

A lean supply chain means fewer intermediaries, better prices for farmers, and a cleaner product for you. Everyone wins — except unnecessary complexity.
