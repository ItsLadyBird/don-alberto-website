Cacay Oil

The Amazon's best-kept secret. Science's most compelling natural facial oil — and communities in Colombia have known it for generations.

Cacay tree · fruits
forest setting
The Plant

Caryodendron
orinocense

The cacay tree grows wild in the Amazon basin, primarily in Colombia, Ecuador, and Venezuela. Unlike cultivated crops, ours is wild-harvested — meaning the tree grows entirely on nature's terms, with no pesticides, no monocultures, no interference.

The seed of the cacay fruit yields a golden oil that has been used by indigenous communities for centuries. Science has only recently confirmed what they always knew: it is extraordinary.

Why it works

The benefits

Natural Retinol

Anti-aging

Retinol (Vitamin A) is the gold standard in anti-aging skincare. Cacay's naturally occurring retinol stimulates collagen production and accelerates cell turnover — visibly reducing fine lines and improving skin texture over time.

Vitamin E

Protection

A potent antioxidant, Vitamin E neutralises free radicals caused by UV exposure and pollution. Cacay's exceptional Vitamin E content helps protect the skin barrier against daily environmental stress.

Linoleic Acid (Ω-6)

Barrier repair

The skin's natural barrier requires linoleic acid to function properly. At ~50%, cacay oil deeply replenishes and repairs moisture barrier integrity — ideal for dry, sensitive, and compromised skin types.

Texture

Lightweight absorption

Despite its dense nutrient profile, cacay oil has a surprisingly light texture. It absorbs rapidly without residue — making it suitable for morning and evening use on all skin types, including oily.

From the Forest

How we
source it

Our cacay oil begins with wild harvest — local communities in Colombia gather fruits from naturally growing trees. No deforestation. No monoculture. The trees remain rooted in their ecosystem, serving birds, insects, and soil as they always have.

After harvest, seeds are cold-pressed without heat or chemical solvents to preserve the full potency of retinol, Vitamin E, and linoleic acid. The result is a raw, unrefined oil — golden, pure, and extraordinarily effective.
